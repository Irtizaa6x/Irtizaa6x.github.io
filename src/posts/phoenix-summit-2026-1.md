I attended **Phoenix Summit 2026** on **26–27 June 2026**, one of Bangladesh's premier cybersecurity conferences held at the **Bangladesh-China Friendship Conference Centre (BCFCC), Dhaka**. The two-day event brought together cybersecurity professionals, researchers, students, government representatives, and industry leaders to discuss the latest trends, challenges, and innovations in digital security.

Throughout the summit, I participated in keynote sessions, technical talks, panel discussions, and networking opportunities covering topics such as cybersecurity, AI security, cloud security, threat intelligence, incident response, and digital resilience. Attending Phoenix Summit 2026 was an inspiring experience that broadened my knowledge, allowed me to connect with experts and fellow enthusiasts, and further strengthened my passion for cybersecurity and emerging technologies.
